# Saddleback Party Rentals: Outreach Playbook

Goal: $7,500–8,500/month gross (≈$5,000 net) from about 16 orders at a $500 average.
Every template below points people to a package or a page that already exists on the site:

- Packages: https://www.saddlebackparty.com (Packages tab)
- Holiday: https://www.saddlebackparty.com/holiday-party-rentals
- Tents: https://www.saddlebackparty.com/wedding-tent-rental

Replace anything in [brackets]. Keep every message short, local and specific.

---

## 1. Review request (text, send the day after pickup)

> Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from Saddleback Party Rentals. Thanks for having us for your [event]! If everything went well, a quick Google review helps our small local business more than anything: [Google review link]. Have a great week!

Target: 50+ Google reviews. Ask after every order and message past customers this week.

## 2. Past-customer re-booking (text, send about 3 weeks before each season)

## 10. Optional winter business: Christmas light installation

This is not on the website yet; add it only if you decide to offer it. Pitch it to past customers in Coto de Caza, Dove Canyon and Robinson Ranch:

> We're also installing holiday lights this season. We design, install, maintain and take it all down in January. Most homes run $[X]–$[Y] depending on roofline. Want a free estimate?

Before you offer it, set up insurance that covers roof and ladder work, and buy commercial-grade clips and LED lights. Aim for 8–10 homes.

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon | Send 5 outreach emails (HOA/PTA/church/partner) and log them in the tracker |
| Tue | Google Business Profile post + reply to reviews |
| Wed | Follow up on last week's emails (one follow-up only) |
| Thu | Facebook/Nextdoor post in 1–2 groups |
| Fri–Sun | Events: take photos of every setup and send review requests after pickup |

**Track weekly:** quotes requested, orders booked, average order, Google review count, and number of repeat or business customers.
