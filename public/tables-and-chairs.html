Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. Delivery, setup, and pickup are included inside our service area. We bring the folding chairs and tables to your address, set them up where you want them, and pick them up at the end of the rental.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Mission Viejo, Rancho Santa Margarita, Lake Forest, Laguna Hills, Laguna Niguel, Coto de Caza, Ladera Ranch, Trabuco Canyon, San Juan Capistrano, Dana Point, and Irvine. Other cities are available for an added delivery fee.

How much do chairs cost?

Folding chairs start at $2.50 each. We rent white and natural wood folding chairs.

How much do tables cost?

Rectangle tables (6 ft and 8 ft) and round tables (48 inch and 60 inch) start at $12 each.

How many people does a round table seat?

48-inch and 60-inch rounds seat 6 to 10 guests each. A 6-foot rectangle table seats 6 to 8.

How far ahead should I book?

Reserve 2 to 3 weeks ahead for spring and summer weekends. Weekday events often need only a few days.

What if it rains?

We watch the forecast and work with you on rescheduling or partial credit.
