Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver and set up?

We bring the chairs and tables to your address. Delivery is $50 to $150 depending on the city and distance, and free on orders over $500. Setup is available on request and quoted with the order. Pickup is scheduled when you book.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Mission Viejo, Rancho Santa Margarita, Lake Forest, Laguna Hills, Laguna Niguel, Coto de Caza, Ladera Ranch, Trabuco Canyon, San Juan Capistrano, Dana Point, and Irvine. Delivery in these cities is $50 to $150 by distance, and free on orders over $500. Other cities are quoted by distance.

How much do chairs cost?

White resin folding chairs start at $2.50 each. Chiavari chairs start at $6 each.

How much do tables cost?

6 ft and 8 ft rectangle tables start at $12 each. A 48-inch round is $14. A 60-inch round is $15.

How many people does a round table seat?

48-inch and 60-inch rounds seat 6 to 10 guests each. A 6-foot rectangle table seats 6 to 8.

How far ahead should I book?

Reserve 2 to 3 weeks ahead for spring and summer weekends. Weekday events often need only a few days.

What if it rains?

We watch the forecast and work with you on rescheduling or partial credit.
