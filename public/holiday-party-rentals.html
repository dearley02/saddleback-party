Frequently Asked

How much does it cost to rent tables and chairs for Thanksgiving?

A 6ft banquet table rents for $12 and a white folding chair for $2.50, so an extra table for 8 is about $32 before linens. Our Holiday Hosting Kit (3 tables, 24 chairs, 3 linens, 2 patio heaters and 2 strands of bistro lights for 24 guests) is $325 with delivery, setup and pickup included.

When should I book holiday party rentals?

Book 2 to 3 weeks ahead for Thanksgiving week and the first three Saturdays of December, which are the busiest days of the season. Patio heaters and tents go first. Weeknight and early-November parties usually need only a few days notice.

Can you deliver the day before Thanksgiving?

Yes. For Thanksgiving we deliver Tuesday or Wednesday and pick up Friday or the following Monday, so you can set the table early and don't have to deal with rentals on the holiday itself.

What if it rains on my holiday party?

Our 10x20 canopies and 20x20 frame tents keep guests dry, and sidewalls ($75) block wind and rain on the sides. If rain is forecast, call us and we'll help you add a tent or move your delivery date.

Do you rent for corporate holiday parties?

Yes. We set up office, warehouse and outdoor corporate holiday parties across South OC with cocktail tables, seating, tents, lighting, heaters and dance floors. We can deliver before business hours and pick up after the event.
